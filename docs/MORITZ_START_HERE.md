## Add an update

1. Sign in at the live workspace using your own account.
2. Choose **Add an update**.
3. Choose the store and **Daily sales**.
4. Add yesterday’s report, a photo, or the text of the message you already send. Say which day it covers and whether cash, GCash, cards, and refunds are included.
5. Press **Save update** and wait for the confirmation. It will appear in **Collection**.

Do this separately for the two stores. Shared overhead can use **Both stores**, with a note explaining how it is shared. Do not enter made-up numbers just to fill a gap; say “unknown.”

**Next update** suggests a first record for the selected store. “First source reviewed” means its context was checked, not that a month is complete or reconciled. **Roadmap** lists phase requirements.

If saving is interrupted, keep the page open and use **Retry the same update**. The details stay held until the server confirms the result. If the login expired, **Sign in and keep my draft** lets you continue as the same person. Avoid closing the browser or restarting the phone during this: offline draft storage is not built yet.

## Further records to collect as available

| Priority | Bring this | Why it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Daily sales for the most recent 30 days, separately by store | Starts a timeline; identifies missing days |
| 2 | Purchase invoices and supplier receipts for the same period | Shows what came in, what it cost, and whether it was paid |
| 3 | Rent, payroll totals, electricity, water, transport, repairs, fees | Starts the running-cost picture |
| 4 | Loyverse products, stock, sales/receipt exports | Reveals SKU names, units, variants, store IDs, and data quality |
| 5 | Photos or a narrated tour of shelves/freezers/preparation areas | Explains real operations and products not captured in the POS |
| 6 | Supplier names, contacts, lead times, order minimums, pack sizes | Makes later ordering useful |

Thirty days is a suggested starting batch, not a requirement to wait. Older reports are welcome. A WhatsApp text export is useful as original evidence; use the relevant business chat and remove unrelated private conversations before uploading. Keep media batches small. The current application stores video and audio; it does not yet transcribe them.

## A daily routine to agree after the first checks

- **At closing:** one sales update for each store, receipts for new purchases/costs, and a note about stockouts, spoilage, or unusual events.
- **When receiving stock:** note supplier, product, pack size, quantity ordered versus received, cost, date, and expiry/batch information where available.
- **When something is unclear:** use **Other**. Say what happened and who can explain it.
- **As operator:** open **Collection**, read the source, and mark it reviewed when the context is understandable. A review note is saved when the review is submitted. For unanswered questions, save a separate **Other** update referencing the source and leave that source pending. Wrong dates/store choices should remain unreviewed; submit a corrected update referencing the original.
