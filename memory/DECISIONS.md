# Decisions

1. **M01 / Start with intake.** The user explicitly asked to begin the feeding phase as quickly as possible. Build a usable multi-user collection and review flow before inventing a dashboard of unavailable business numbers. Keep profitability unknown.
2. **M02 / Preserve the existing POS and procurement work.** Read the Loyverse and Sausage projects without modifying them. Keep Loyverse as initial source for POS sales/stock; later verify and integrate ProcurePilot rather than rebuilding its purchasing engine.
3. **M03 / Portable single-workspace pilot.** React/TypeScript + FastAPI + SQLite/files, served from one origin; container packaging for a later chosen host. This reuses the language stack of the procurement app while isolating database ownership. Multi-business SaaS is deferred.
4. **M04 / Design selected by delegation.** The user requested Design OS and Protocol OS autopilot with strong visual direction requirements. Generate three options and select Tropical Observatory. No explicit owner approval was fabricated; record the agent-selected verdict and preserve alternatives.
5. **M05 / Roles reflect actual operating responsibilities.** Technical owner administers the system. Moritz reviews operational sources. Staff submit and see their own updates for assigned stores. No staff developer tooling or shared owner account.
6. **M06 / AI is explicit and bounded.** Build optional text extraction through OpenRouter, disabled until key/model/enabling flag are configured. No live spending, OCR claims, background autonomous actions, or fabricated output. Models produce proposals; quotes, schema, and human review constrain their use.
7. **M07 / Private source repository.** “Put it on GitHub” authorizes creating/pushing a private repository under the existing authenticated personal account. Business uploads, secrets, runtime databases and local credentials are excluded. The domain/server are supplied later, so public deployment is not part of this initial action.
8. **M08 / Durable context.** Onboard Protocol OS at involvement 1 (Autopilot), with `memory/` as brain and an explicit initial-milestone scope. Do not continue arbitrary backlog work merely because a context window resets. No Orrery integration was requested or enabled.
9. **M09 / No legal or financial assumptions.** Corporation type, investor participation, tax treatment, costing, and accounting periods require real documents and professional confirmation. Geography transcriptions remain flagged; Azure Sky is not seeded as a third store.

10. **M10 / Preserve the island; refine its surrounding experience (9 September).** The owner explicitly liked the island and requested the Tropical Observatory direction be preserved. Reuse the existing transparent artwork and palette; extend the game-like setting through route stops, editorial chapter copy and subtle native CSS detail. No new asset generation or mockup round was needed for this refinement. Final refined-screen owner review is still open.
11. **M11 / Guide from evidence, never simulated completion.** First-source suggestions use permitted records per selected store and category; shared reports cannot fill individual store slots. A source review does not certify finances or unlock a phase. The roadmap names owners, acceptance requirements, risks and responses.
12. **M12 / Treat interrupted saves as uncertain.** Preserve the exact pending submission and idempotency key for retry, allow same-account reauthentication without unmounting the draft, and request an unload warning. No sensitive drafts are persisted to browser localStorage. Offline capture remains a separate feature.
13. **M13 / Recommend a subdomain, subject to host selection.** `ops.thesausageguy.shop` can serve the separate private workspace without buying another domain or merging the public app. Public DNS currently points to Porkbun/Vercel; these routing facts do not establish credential access. No hostname or DNS mutation was approved/performed.
14. **M14 / Validate the actual package in CI.** The local Docker daemon is unavailable, so the private repository's Linux checks now build/test the image with a disposable volume. A passing package/restart/backup check reduces deployment risk but does not replace the real host's HTTPS, phone or restore acceptance.
15. **M15 / Plain product copy (9 September, explicit owner correction).** Remove invitation/account-reset notices, motivational taglines and unsolicited employee coaching. Use functional labels and concise facts. Keep the island and visual direction. Optional Help can explain tasks; infrastructure detail belongs in owner controls or internal docs. This supersedes M10's chapter-copy choice, not its visual direction. Give the owner the concrete next action instead of links to generic guides.
16. **M16 / Hostname confirmed; contact received.** The owner selected `ops.thesausageguy.shop` and supplied Moritz's email. Contact and sent-email details are stored in private `.data/moritz-handoff.json`, not Git. Server selection/access is still pending; do not ask for the same email or hostname again. This supersedes M13's unconfirmed-hostname status.
17. **M17 / Specific operational email authorization.** The owner asked to send Moritz the initial data request. Sent one concise email through the signed-in Gmail and verified "Message sent". It requests a dated sales report from each store and a receipt, then costs and a Loyverse export, and explicitly says online login is still being set up. No credentials or business records were sent. This authorizes that message, not recurring mail or customer outreach.
18. **M18 / Vercel selected.** The owner wants Vercel and does not want to manage server/IP details. Created its separate project. Proposed Neon Free Postgres plus private Vercel Blob; current SQLite/uploads cannot be deployed unchanged. Neon setup awaits the owner's login/terms review; no connected storage or paid plan confirmed. `docs/VERCEL.md` records remaining implementation and checks. Hosting does not connect AI automatically.

19. **M19 / Selected cloud target authorized and implemented (9 September).** The owner completed the requested Neon terms and said continue. Provisioned Neon Free and private Blob under the existing Vercel project, separate individual accounts and ops DNS/HTTPS. This specific target supersedes the historical pending-host state. No related repository was changed and no paid plan was upgraded. Existing Pro Blob usage is billed; a 1 GiB reservation quota is not monetary approval or a provider dollar cap.
20. **M20 / Immutable cloud originals and verified finalization.** Use Postgres plus direct file-specific private upload grants and server size/SHA checks, preserving exact-payload retries and transactional finalization. A direct PUT response is not record acceptance. Python/Node official SDK boundaries are explicit; original files and credentials never enter Git.
21. **M21 / Recovery evidence before pilot claims.** The live source was backed up encrypted outside Vercel/Neon and restored to a separate schema/private prefix. Both logins, review and original hashes passed; sessions were excluded. Removed only identified synthetic validation resources, retaining encrypted evidence privately. The next gate is a real phone/network upload and second-account review. A one-time drill is not an installed recurring backup operation.

22. **M22 / First real exports ingested (11 September).** The owner supplied two root CSVs and explicitly asked to organize, ingest and analyze them. Moved originals unchanged into ignored private intake storage, uploaded them plus a separate derived review to the selected live collection, and retained both-account visibility without marking human review. No business data enters Git; root CSV files are additionally ignored.
23. **M23 / Read export semantics before business conclusions.** Treat signed refunds once, shared Handle fields as variant metadata, variable price as variable and stock as a dated snapshot. Require unit/cost/coverage confirmation and SKU-level receipt detail before product/profit decisions. Raw receipt descriptions are not treated as reliable SKU sale lines. The measurement plan connects each future number to an operating decision.
24. **M24 / Connectors deferred; no inferred installation.** The screenshot describes optional app-to-app automation services. Existing CSV intake is sufficient. Later evaluate a direct read-only Loyverse interface or connector only after a concrete workflow, verified field/store mappings and approved cost/access. No connector, AI provider or scheduled sync was enabled.
25. **M25 / Stay in feeding and digestion (11 September, explicit owner correction).** Collect, reconcile and explain the business records before changing the business. Product placement, bundles, pricing/marketing experiments and purchasing changes are deferred. Updated the measurement plan and added a new live review that supersedes the old commercial next-step suggestions while retaining the original report.
26. **M26 / Supplied Loyverse access verified, manual reads only.** The owner provided the account token and asked to continue intake. Verified official GET-only store/merchant/catalogue/inventory/receipt access and saved exact response originals privately. The account covers The Sausage Guy only; health-store access remains unresolved. Credential permissions are broad; read-only use does not make it a provider-restricted token. No web runtime connection, schedule, webhook, POS write or related-repository change was made.
27. **M27 / Reconcile sources before combining anything.** API refund magnitudes and signed CSV refunds use different conventions. Normalize API refunds once, preserve exact money, compare stable account/store/receipt/line/variant identities and keep API-only records separate. Matched timestamps align at UTC+8 but do not certify timezone settings or complete trading days. The initial comparator rejects unvalidated tax/tip/surcharge/cancellation semantics. Current catalogue costs are not substitutes for validated historical unit costs.

28. **M28 / Items page reads the Loyverse catalogue on request, never on a schedule (15 September).** The owner asked for an Items tab in the dashboard listing items, stock, optimal stock and variants, synced with the Loyverse API. Implemented `server/loyverse.py` plus the Items page as a GET-only reader of `/merchant/`, `/stores`, `/categories`, `/items` and `/inventory`. A person presses refresh; the normalized result is stored as one dated snapshot. No schedule, webhook, background worker, receipt/sales endpoint or POS write. It stays off until `SH_LOYVERSE_ENABLED=1`, so the credential in `.data/loyverse-access.json` still does not authorize replay by itself. A cooldown and a daily cap bound the account's 300-per-300-seconds limit; failures keep the previous snapshot and never persist a provider body. This supersedes M24's deferral for the catalogue read only: receipts, connectors, scheduled sync and any POS write remain deferred and unauthorized.
29. **M29 / An absent figure is rendered as absent.** Untracked items, composite items without production, variants with no inventory level and unset optimal/low-stock targets are each labelled distinctly and never shown as zero, while a genuine counted zero is preserved exactly. Quantities and money stay exact decimals; a shortfall is computed only when both the count and the target are known. Loyverse defaults a variant cost to 0.00, so a zero cost is displayed but never treated as a verified unit cost, and the page derives no stock value, margin or profit.
30. **M30 / No Loyverse store is attributed to a shop without an explicit mapping.** `SH_LOYVERSE_STORE_MAP` is the only way a Loyverse store becomes The Sausage Guy or Natural Mind Health. An unmapped store is shown to the owner as unattributed and hidden from store operators, and a mapping entry this account does not return is reported as having no effect. Natural Mind Health's account question stays open rather than being answered by assuming the single returned store.

**Assumptions recorded for M28 (owner may overrule):** the item list follows the review roles rather than submission access, so owner and store operator can read and refresh it while store team accounts are refused by the API; store operators may refresh because the read is GET-only, bounded and audited; and the live account has not been read through this new reader — switching the connection on is an owner action.

31. **M31 / Production Loyverse catalogue connection authorized and made (16 September).** The owner asked to publish the Items page and then to connect Loyverse so items appear. Added `SH_LOYVERSE_ENABLED`, `SH_LOYVERSE_TOKEN` and `SH_LOYVERSE_STORE_MAP` to the Vercel Production environment and redeployed. This authorizes the on-request catalogue read only. It does not authorize a schedule, a webhook, any POS write, receipt or sales reading, or AI. The credential is a broad personal access token living in the serverless runtime; that risk was stated and accepted. Both the Vercel and Loyverse tokens were pasted into a chat transcript during setup and should be rotated.
32. **M32 / The store mapping was read from the source, not assumed.** A GET to /stores returned exactly one store, literally named The Sausage Guy, so `dee620ec-081c-4094-833a-09df447b01de` maps to `sausage`. This confirms M30 rather than weakening it: Natural Mind Health still has no account of its own, and nothing in the workspace attributes any row to it.
33. **M33 / Optimal stock is empty at the source.** The live account has 451 variants and **zero** optimal stock targets, while 134 variants are at or below a low-stock threshold. The Optimal column and the shortfall calculation work, but they will show "Not set" and no shortfall until someone sets those targets in the Loyverse back office. Do not fill that gap with a guess, a reorder formula or a default.
