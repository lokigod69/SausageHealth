# Design round 01 · Built-in image-generation tool

## observatory

Use case: ui-mockup. High-fidelity desktop web app UI, one screen, flat frontal view, 1536x1024 landscape. Sausage Health is a private operating hub for The Sausage Guy and Natural Mind Health, two small shops on Panglao island. Real content. Left sidebar brand 'sausage health', nav Overview, Collection, Store journal, Roadmap, Agent team. Header 'Your island, in focus.' Main hero 'Good things start with a clear picture.' and 'Collect the everyday. Understand the business. Grow with confidence.' Primary button 'Add your first update'. A status label 'PHASE 01 / COLLECT'. Below hero, today's three tasks: 'Share yesterday’s sales', 'Add a supplier receipt', 'Introduce your stores'. Below three understated counters: '0 updates collected', '2 stores connected by a shared plan', 'Profit: awaiting records'. Never make up revenue. Attractive layout with exceptional typography and a generous hero illustration. Single complete screen, no collages, no tilted device, no lorem ipsum. Direction: tropical observatory. Deep pine green #102621 background, warm ivory text, golden amber buttons, muted mint lines. Editorial serif headline with modern sans labels. Large detailed isometric pixel-art tropical island to hero right with exactly two small storefronts, palms, warm glowing windows, paths connecting stores, tiny lagoon, textured square pixels. Art breathes into dark background, beautifully composed high-end field station. Small pixel sparkle marks. Restrained hairlines, elegant rounded 18px panels. One strong amber action, thoughtful asymmetry. Beautiful, calm, premium.
