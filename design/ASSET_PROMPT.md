# Island illustration

Tool: built-in image-generation tool. Original generation retained in the Codex image directory; project copy is `public/art/island.png`. No raster editing or alpha stripping was performed.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a standalone production illustration for a premium private retail web app, an exquisite isometric pixel-art tropical island at dusk. Exactly two distinct small independent storefronts sit at the foreground left and right of the island, a warm cream and amber butcher/deli with striped awning, and a botanical green health-food shop. On a gentle hill behind them is a small domed observatory with one glowing golden window, symbolizing understanding. Connected by small sandy cobblestone pathways, lush textured palms, cacao plants, tiny amber path lights, bright teal shallow water hugging the island's edge, a little wooden dock. Crisp deliberate square pixels with rich fine detail and romantic handcrafted atmosphere, 1990s adventure-game world with modern lighting, deep emerald and bronze with warm honey light and ivory stone. A fully contained island silhouette with irregular organic edges, floating as an isolated cutout on a genuinely TRANSPARENT background (alpha channel), transparent outside shoreline, no square opaque background. All objects fully visible inside frame, generous breathing room around silhouette, horizontal 3:2 aspect composition. No interface, absolutely no lettering or logos or text, no people, no additional stores, no caption. The asset will overlap a dark pine dashboard but must retain real transparency. Medium distance isometric view; tall palms around the edges; island fills 88% of image. Render beautifully textured illustrated pixels, avoid smooth 3D plastic.
